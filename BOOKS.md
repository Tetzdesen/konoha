# Reading list

Each Konoha agent encodes the core principles of these works as checklists, written in our own words. The agents are a starting point; the books are the real training. Read them.

## Coordination — Hokage (`konoha` skill)
- Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais. *Team Topologies*. IT Revolution, 2019.
- Frederick P. Brooks Jr. *The Mythical Man-Month*, anniversary ed. Addison-Wesley, 1995.
- Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim. *Accelerate*. IT Revolution, 2018.

## Product — `konoha-product`
- Marty Cagan. *Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love*, 2nd ed. Wiley, 2017.
- Teresa Torres. *Continuous Discovery Habits*. Product Talk, 2021.
- Jeff Patton. *User Story Mapping*. O'Reilly, 2014.

## Requirements — `konoha-requirements`
- Karl Wiegers, Joy Beatty. *Software Requirements*, 3rd ed. Microsoft Press, 2013.
- Gojko Adzic. *Specification by Example*. Manning, 2011.
- Mike Cohn. *User Stories Applied*. Addison-Wesley, 2004.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 — Requirements engineering.
- ISO/IEC 25010 — Product quality model.

## Architecture — `konoha-architect`
- Mark Richards, Neal Ford. *Fundamentals of Software Architecture*. O'Reilly, 2020.
- Eric Evans. *Domain-Driven Design*. Addison-Wesley, 2003.
- Robert C. Martin. *Clean Architecture*. Prentice Hall, 2017.
- John Ousterhout. *A Philosophy of Software Design*, 2nd ed. Yaknyam Press, 2021.
- Michael Nygard. "Documenting Architecture Decisions" (ADRs), 2011.

## Data — `konoha-data`
- Martin Kleppmann. *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*. O'Reilly, 2017.
- Ramez Elmasri, Shamkant Navathe. *Fundamentals of Database Systems*, 7th ed. Pearson, 2016.
- Bill Karwin. *SQL Antipatterns*. Pragmatic Bookshelf, 2010.

## UX — `konoha-ux`
- Steve Krug. *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited*. New Riders, 2014.
- Don Norman. *The Design of Everyday Things*, revised ed. Basic Books, 2013.
- Jakob Nielsen. "10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design". Nielsen Norman Group.
- W3C. *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2*.

## Development — `konoha-developer`
- Andrew Hunt, David Thomas. *The Pragmatic Programmer*, 20th anniversary ed. Addison-Wesley, 2019.
- Steve McConnell. *Code Complete*, 2nd ed. Microsoft Press, 2004.
- John Ousterhout. *A Philosophy of Software Design*, 2nd ed. Yaknyam Press, 2021.
- Joshua Bloch. *Effective Java*, 3rd ed. Addison-Wesley, 2018.
- Kent Beck. *Test-Driven Development: By Example*. Addison-Wesley, 2002.

## Review — `konoha-reviewer`
- Martin Fowler. *Refactoring*, 2nd ed. Addison-Wesley, 2018.
- Michael Feathers. *Working Effectively with Legacy Code*. Prentice Hall, 2004.
- Robert C. Martin. *Clean Code*. Prentice Hall, 2008.

## QA — `konoha-qa`
- Vladimir Khorikov. *Unit Testing Principles, Practices, and Patterns*. Manning, 2020.
- Lisa Crispin, Janet Gregory. *Agile Testing*. Addison-Wesley, 2009.
- Steve Freeman, Nat Pryce. *Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by Tests*. Addison-Wesley, 2009.

## Security — `konoha-security`
- Adam Shostack. *Threat Modeling: Designing for Security*. Wiley, 2014.
- Dan Bergh Johnsson, Daniel Deogun, Daniel Sawano. *Secure by Design*. Manning, 2019.
- OWASP Top 10 and OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS), latest editions.

## Privacy — `konoha-privacy`
- Brasil. Lei nº 13.709/2018 — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (LGPD).
- Ann Cavoukian. *Privacy by Design: The 7 Foundational Principles*, 2009.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## DevOps — `konoha-devops`
- Jez Humble, David Farley. *Continuous Delivery*. Addison-Wesley, 2010.
- Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, John Willis, Nicole Forsgren. *The DevOps Handbook*, 2nd ed. IT Revolution, 2021.

## SRE — `konoha-sre`
- Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, Niall Richard Murphy (eds.). *Site Reliability Engineering*. O'Reilly, 2016.
- Michael Nygard. *Release It!*, 2nd ed. Pragmatic Bookshelf, 2018.
- Charity Majors, Liz Fong-Jones, George Miranda. *Observability Engineering*. O'Reilly, 2022.

## Performance — `konoha-performance`
- Brendan Gregg. *Systems Performance*, 2nd ed. Addison-Wesley, 2020.

## Documentation — `konoha-docs`
- Jared Bhatti, Zachary Sarah Corleissen, Jen Lambourne, David Nunez, Heidi Waterhouse. *Docs for Developers*. Apress, 2021.
- Daniele Procida. *Diátaxis* — a systematic approach to technical documentation. diataxis.fr
